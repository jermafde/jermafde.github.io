Reconstruction of 6D Phase Space

Tested capabilities of machine learning using a ResNet18 CNN to predict particle beam average initial momentum directly from 2D spatial distribution histograms captured by virtual beam detectors.

  • Data Pipeline: Constructed a C++ macro, using G4Beamline simulations across 125-185 MeV/c, with 5 MeV/c increments, as input.
  • Optimization: Densified training sets in 2 MeV/c intervals around most innacurate testing points.
  • Result: Achieved a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 0.966 MeV/c across test distributions.
